About

I'm a data analyst with an MSc in Data Science from the University of Hertfordshire, focused on the commercial side of the business: stock, customers, orders and the money that moves between them. I like starting from the question a manager is actually asking and working back to the query and the dashboard that answer it.

Alongside analysis I run @bala.explains, where I teach Excel, SQL and Power BI to people starting from zero. Explaining a JOIN to a beginner in 30 seconds has made me much better at explaining a finding to a stakeholder in one slide.

I start with the data itself. In the Zepto analysis, the standard checks flagged 4 bad rows; a structural check found that more than half the dataset was duplicated. I'd rather catch that before a number reaches a dashboard. Every project here is on GitHub, including the SQL and the decisions behind it.
